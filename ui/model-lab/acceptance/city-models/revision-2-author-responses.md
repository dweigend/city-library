## Further findings during revision 2

- **Shared host: Enter in a live field restarts the model.** Live controls sit inside the setup form, so pressing Enter submits it and runs Setup, discarding progress. Observed with Economy: after a live edit confirmed with Enter, the model returned to month 0. This is existing shared behavior of `ui/svelte/Workbench.svelte` and affects every model; it was not changed here because a host change needs its own decision and checks across all models. Browser runs confirm live edits with Tab instead. **Open for the coordinator.**
- Economy with spending fractions above 0.5 has resting bands of more than one job because jobs are whole numbers; this is documented in the contract rather than hidden.
