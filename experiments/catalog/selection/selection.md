# Selected models

The catalog scope is exactly 200 models. These models cover urban systems and the wider mechanisms needed for reusable simulations and games. Each source path and slug is fixed by the [selection manifest](selection.json).

The [acceptance ledger](../progress.json) records current implementation and verification. The [model source index](../../../docs/research/netlogo-web/INDEX.md) explains the scientific questions, source provenance and relevant literature. Neither selection nor literature research establishes implementation acceptance.

## Selection principles

The collection includes all eleven Urban Suite models, five core reference models and complementary mechanisms from ecology, genetics, disease, physics, mathematics and computing. Related models belong where they exercise different rules: lane changes and car following, goal-directed movement, spatial formation, interventions, learning and alternative topologies. Presentation-only variants and large families of near-identical elementary rules are outside this selection.

## Model groups

Topic groups organize the selection; they do not define implementation order or acceptance status. The required public contracts and source-specific dependencies determine the next work.

## Maintain the scope

Keep the selected membership, source paths, slugs and source hashes unchanged unless a new scope is explicitly agreed. Update implementation and verification through the acceptance ledger. For required source, headless, browser and independent checks, follow the [model acceptance rules](../../../docs/development/netlogo-model-acceptance.md).

The original selection record and checksum manifest are retained as [source records](../../../evidence/catalog/selection/selection.md). Their paths describe the original checkout; [location metadata](../../../evidence/locations.json) resolves them to retained files. The JSON and CSV manifests remain in this directory.
