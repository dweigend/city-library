Implemented adaptation: readable headless TypeScript composes public Agent/World/Graph/Random operations, with separate typed UI projections. Mulberry replaces NetLogo's RNG; setup headings, spring-layout randomness and order-independent timer-phase shuffles are omitted. Nearest-unconnected topology generation precedes independent view layout. Sender membership is frozen before spreading; newly infected nodes can still receive timed recovery checks in that tick. Recovery and resistance retain the original integer random(100) comparisons, including quantized fractional recovery chances. The setup population, degree and outbreak sliders are setup-only and dependent maxima are enforced headlessly. Additional atomic state/connection fixtures preserve scientific time and RNG. Observations and initial-plus-latest-1000 completed-step histories are detached. Network layout, palette and glyph sizes are independent view choices and consume no model randomness.

Implementation does not establish target/browser, baseline, scientific-research or independent acceptance. These remain coordinator work, with source-specific contracts in [the network contracts](../../docs/development/selected-network-contracts.md). The separately owned research track supplies scientific literature evidence; none was researched here.
