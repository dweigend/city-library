# Radical Polymerization with Chains

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Radical Polymerization with Chains investigates how local radical reactions generate explicit polymer-chain structure. Radical agents initiate reactions with nearby monomers or radicals, while bonded chain members remain connected; movement and reaction order determine which encounters can occur. Explains radical initiation, propagation and termination mechanisms [1]. Provides the molecular-size-distribution perspective, while its branching and gelation assumptions are not all present here [2]. Provides the local constrained-chain modeling context relevant to an explicit lattice polymer representation [3]. The source is a spatial lattice model rather than a calibrated industrial polymerization process; chain length statistics depend on encounter geometry, mobility and termination choices. The simulation illustrates why counting reacted monomers alone misses connectivity and the distribution of chain sizes. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
