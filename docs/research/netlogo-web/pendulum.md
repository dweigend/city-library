# Pendulum

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Pendulum investigates how pendulum length determines the period in the small-angle approximation. An ideal bob on a massless string follows an explicitly sinusoidal angular trajectory, with angular frequency given by the square root of gravity divided by length. Reviews the physical richness and approximations of the pendulum [1]. Provides a nonlinear analytical comparison beyond the small-angle regime [2]. Supplies the classical force-and-motion background; the cited record is a modern translation [3]. The original baseline omits air resistance and uses an analytic harmonic formula rather than solving the full nonlinear pendulum; changing bob mass does not change that formula. The simulation illustrates phase relations between displacement, velocity and acceleration while exposing the approximation behind apparent energy conservation. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
