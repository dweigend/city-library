# Simple Kinetics 2

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Simple Kinetics 2 investigates how a reversible association reaction responds to changed concentrations. Two reactant agents can associate into one product, while products can dissociate; adjustable forward and reverse event rules produce a dynamic balance. Provides a rigorous stochastic-reaction simulation reference for finite populations [1]. Clarifies the relation between reaction propensities and event timing [2]. Develops stochastic chemical kinetics beyond deterministic concentration equations [3]. This spatial teaching model does not automatically satisfy the well-mixed assumptions or continuous-time event law of the cited algorithms; reaction probabilities require calibration before being called physical rate constants. The simulation illustrates why equilibrium can shift after a perturbation while forward and reverse microscopic events continue. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
