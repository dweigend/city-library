# Buffer

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Buffer investigates how a weak acid and its conjugate base resist added acid or base. Added hydronium reacts with conjugate base, while added hydroxide consumes weak acid; finite reservoirs determine whether the mixture can continue resisting changes. Connects acid strength and conjugate-species balance to preservation of neutrality [1]. Develops measurement and mathematical interpretation of buffer capacity [2]. Provides the proton-donor and proton-acceptor mechanism behind the two neutralizing reactions [3]. This teaching model does not establish physiological safety ranges or calibrated solution activities; buffering has finite capacity and depends on species amounts as well as dissociation balance. The simulation illustrates why a buffered solution responds weakly to some additions but changes sharply after a reservoir is depleted. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
