# HIV

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do partnership duration, testing, and safer-sex assumptions change transmission opportunities? NetLogo HIV forms and dissolves couples in an isolated population and distinguishes unknown from known infection. The served model assumes that people who know they are infected consistently practice safer sex. Research on sexual-contact structure [1], condom effectiveness [2], and early antiretroviral treatment [3] frames both its mechanisms and omissions. Testing behavior is represented through simple tendencies, and the model does not reconstruct contemporary treatment access, adherence, or all transmission routes. It illustrates how local partnerships and information can affect population patterns. It does not provide a calibrated estimate of HIV risk or demonstrate the effectiveness of an actual public-health program.
