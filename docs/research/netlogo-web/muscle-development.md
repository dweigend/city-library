# Muscle Development

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do training frequency and recovery assumptions change simulated muscle development? NetLogo Muscle Development links weight lifting, sleep, and simplified hormonal balance to changes in muscle. Hypertrophy mechanisms [1] and research on resistance training [2] provide scientific context. Evidence questioning a simple link between transient circulating hormones and protein synthesis [3] is particularly important when interpreting the model's explanatory story. Its hormone and recovery variables compress many biological pathways into illustrative rules and are not personalized physiological measurements. The simulation can expose interactions and delayed consequences within those rules. It does not establish an optimal training plan or demonstrate that changing one modeled hormone directly produces the depicted amount of muscle growth.
