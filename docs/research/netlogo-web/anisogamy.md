# Anisogamy

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Under what assumptions can two gamete sizes evolve from an initially less differentiated population? NetLogo Anisogamy explores reproductive investment, gamete encounters, and offspring survival through spatially explicit agents. Foundational gamete-dimorphism theory [1], the model authors' thought-experiment study [2], and a game-theoretic analysis [3] frame the evolutionary question. The Info presents the model as a tool for challenging and comparing explanations, rather than a universal origin story. Discrete time, spatial encounters, and the specified relation between gamete investment and success constrain interpretation. Emerging size differences illustrate one set of evolutionary consequences. They do not establish that every biological lineage followed the same path or that the chosen survival and encounter rules are empirically calibrated.
