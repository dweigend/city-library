# DLA

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo DLA investigates how diffusing particles can produce branched aggregate geometry. Wandering particles become permanently attached when they reach the seeded cluster; replacement walkers maintain the incoming population, while their turning angle changes transport. Introduces diffusion-limited aggregation as a kinetic growth model [1]. Examines irreversible aggregation and the formation of fractal clusters [2]. Critically discusses diffusion-limited aggregation and its kinetic interpretation [3]. Sticking is irreversible and the lattice, launch positions and persistent versus random motion influence growth; visual resemblance to coral or lightning is not validation. The simulation illustrates screening of interior regions and preferential growth of exposed branches. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
