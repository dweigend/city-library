# Turtles Circling

Status: complete. Scientific research checked 2026-11-05.

What collective shape appears when independent circular motions change radius? NetLogo Turtles Circling starts agents around a common circle, then repeatedly advances and turns each agent according to the selected radius and speed. Changing the radius during motion alters each agent's local trajectory while the group traces a new aggregate pattern. Curve geometry [1] relates turning to curvature; the pursuit constructions in [2] provide comparable examples of paths generated by simple motion rules; graphics methods [3] explain coordinate and trajectory representations. The agents do not attract one another or enforce a collective boundary. Finite steps approximate the intended circular motion, and changing parameters need not preserve the original center. The model illustrates the distinction between individual paths and an emergent visible envelope, rather than a physical mechanism maintaining a ring.
