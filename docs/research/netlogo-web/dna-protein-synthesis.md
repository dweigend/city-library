# DNA Protein Synthesis

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do sequence changes alter the products of gene expression? NetLogo DNA Protein Synthesis depicts DNA, transcription into mRNA, and translation involving tRNA and amino-acid chains. Users can compare substitutions, insertions, and deletions and observe their modeled consequences. The central dogma [1], code-deciphering experiments [2], and genetic reading-frame work [3] provide scientific context. The served model is a curricular representation of selected molecular steps, not a complete simulation of folding, splicing, quality control, or cellular regulation. It illustrates why a sequence change can have different consequences depending on its position and type. Its animations do not establish actual protein function, the clinical significance of a variant, or quantitative synthesis rates.
