# Rebellion

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 116 words

The scientific question is whether local risk perceptions and grievances generate bursts of overt protest. In NetLogo's Rebellion model, citizens compare hardship-based grievance with perceived arrest risk while moving police arrest active rebels. The selected literature connects this mechanism to threshold participation and repression: the explicitly acknowledged civil-violence model supplies citizen-police dynamics [1]; participation thresholds connect individual incentives to collective mobilization [2]; informational cascades provide a historically grounded alternative protest mechanism [3]. Legitimacy, risk aversion and visibility are stylized inputs; the model cannot forecast protest from a grievance slider alone. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
