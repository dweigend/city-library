# Plant Growth

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can a growing structure concentrate effort where resources are available? NetLogo Plant Growth creates leaf-like and root-like cells that collect different resources but require both to survive. Directionally biased exchange of sugar and water helps sustain productive branches while unproductive regions can fail. Root–shoot allocation theory [1], algorithmic plant architecture [2], and root-plasticity research [3] provide scientific comparisons. The Info explicitly says the model is not intended to be biologically realistic. Its resource flows and cell addition rules are exploratory abstractions rather than plant vascular physiology. Adaptive-looking growth illustrates consequences of these rules; it does not establish optimal allocation, realistic developmental signaling, or a quantitative prediction of yield in a particular environment.
