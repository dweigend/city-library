# Rabbits Grass Weeds

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do food quality and regrowth affect a population of consumers? NetLogo Rabbits Grass Weeds lets wandering rabbits eat two plant resources, gain energy, reproduce, and die when energy is exhausted. Grass and weeds can differ in growth and energetic value. Consumer-resource feedback [1], resource competition [2], and feeding-response theory [3] frame the scientific questions this microworld illustrates. Its random movement and patch-level eating rules replace many biological details, including explicit plant physiology, age structure, and measured feeding responses. Changes in population trajectories show consequences of the declared resource and energy assumptions. They should not be read as calibrated predictions of rabbit carrying capacity, agricultural yield, or stable coexistence in a particular habitat.
