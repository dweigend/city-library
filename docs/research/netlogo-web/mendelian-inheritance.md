# Mendelian Inheritance

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do parental genotypes produce predictable offspring ratios while individual births remain uncertain? NetLogo Mendelian Inheritance lets users breed a male and female mouse with homozygous or heterozygous states and observe offspring coat colors. Mendel's experiments, available in a scholarly translation [1], ground segregation; mouse coat-color genetics [2] and molecular agouti research [3] show the biological detail behind the example. The simulation reduces inheritance to the chosen dominant–recessive trait and simplified reproduction. Expected ratios describe repeated sampling, not a requirement that every small litter has exact proportions. The model illustrates allele transmission and genotype–phenotype distinctions, while omitting much of the multi-locus regulation and developmental biology underlying real coat colors.
