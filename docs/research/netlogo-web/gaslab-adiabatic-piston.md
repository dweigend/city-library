# GasLab Adiabatic Piston

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo GasLab Adiabatic Piston investigates how molecular impacts exchange energy with a movable loaded piston. Elastic gas particles strike a weighted moving piston, changing both particle velocities and piston motion, while the containing walls exchange no heat. Provides the microscopic collision basis for momentum transfer [1]. Analyzes the time evolution and thermodynamic subtleties of the adiabatic-piston problem [2]. Provides a computational kinetic reference for interpreting finite-particle collision dynamics [3]. The model is two-dimensional and uses local collision detection; a small gas and simplified boundaries do not automatically realize quasistatic macroscopic thermodynamics. The simulation illustrates mechanical pressure, piston fluctuations and energy exchange through work without supplied heat. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
