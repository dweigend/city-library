# GenEvo 1 Genetic Switch

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can the same cell switch gene expression in response to an environmental resource? NetLogo GenEvo 1 Genetic Switch represents the lac operon through DNA regions, regulatory proteins, RNA polymerase, lactose, and expressed enzymes. Jacob and Monod's regulatory framework [1], lactose-network multistability [2], and single-cell switching research [3] provide the scientific context. The model makes binding and transcription events visible while compressing molecular transport, concentrations, and reaction kinetics. It illustrates regulation through interactions rather than a prewritten instruction to turn a gene on. The resulting switch is a consequence of selected rules, not proof of quantitative agreement with every bacterial strain or experimental condition, nor a complete account of lac-operon regulation.
