# Interstitial Diffusion

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Interstitial Diffusion investigates how small atoms migrate through unoccupied sites inside a host lattice. Interstitial atoms attempt random nearest-site jumps, succeed only when the destination is empty, and may encounter controlled surface concentrations. Studies interstitial diffusion coefficients and their atomic-scale interpretation [1]. Supplies the macroscopic concentration-gradient framework for the plotted profile [2]. Places interstitial jumps and boundary concentrations within solid-state transport theory [3]. The host lattice remains fixed and jump probabilities do not encode a complete material-specific energy landscape; high occupancies exceed typical dilute interstitial conditions. The simulation illustrates concentration smoothing, exclusion and the influence of maintained boundary reservoirs. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
