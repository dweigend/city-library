# Virus

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

What allows an infection to persist when susceptibility, immunity, births, and deaths interact? NetLogo Virus moves people among susceptible, infectious, and immune states, with demographic renewal and disease-related mortality. The Info explicitly cites research on viral perpetuation [1]; classical epidemic theory [2] and community-size evidence [3] provide further context. Contact emerges from random movement rather than a measured social network, and immunity duration and recovery are controlled by stylized parameters. The model illustrates how replenishment of susceptible individuals can change outbreak trajectories compared with a closed epidemic. Persistence or extinction in one run does not establish a population threshold for a real virus, empirical calibration, or a reliable forecast of future outbreaks.
