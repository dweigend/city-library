# Prisoner's Dilemma Basic

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 121 words

The scientific question is whether individually attractive choices can produce mutually inferior outcomes. In NetLogo's Prisoner's Dilemma Basic model, two players choose confession or silence and receive prison sentences from a fixed payoff matrix. The selected literature connects this mechanism to dominance and strategic interdependence: equilibrium supplies the formal benchmark for mutual best responses [1]; repeated-game strategies demonstrate why one-shot reasoning has limits [2]; reciprocity changes the conditions under which cooperation persists [3]. One-shot play, common payoffs and no binding communication define the dilemma; results cannot directly predict behavior under trust, repetition or social preferences. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
