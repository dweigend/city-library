# Blood Sugar Regulation

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can interacting organs stabilize blood glucose after disturbances? NetLogo Blood Sugar Regulation represents liver and pancreatic tissue as patches and glucose, insulin, and glucagon as moving molecules. Local interactions implement opposing hormonal responses rather than explicit blood vessels. Quantitative glucose regulation [1], glucagon physiology [2], and beta-cell secretion research [3] support the model's scientific framing. Its particle diffusion and simplified cell rules compress circulation, receptor signaling, and metabolic pathways into visible events. The simulation illustrates negative feedback and the distinction between increasing and decreasing glucose availability. It is not a calibrated physiological model, cannot determine a person's insulin sensitivity, and should not be interpreted as evidence for a clinical intervention or dosing decision.
