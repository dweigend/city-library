# Fruit Wars

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 128 words

The scientific question is whether larger benefits from joint foraging can favor less violent inherited strategies. In NetLogo's Fruit Wars model, foragers gather fruit, reproduce and pass on traits controlling cooperation, threats, fighting and flight, subject to energetic tradeoffs. The selected literature connects this mechanism to evolutionary conflict and mutual benefit: evolutionary stability formalizes competition between conflict strategies [1]; cooperative interactions can resist exploitation under specified conditions [2]; distinct cooperation mechanisms delimit generalizations from a single payoff environment [3]. The genetic encoding and collaboration bonus define a particular artificial ecology; they do not measure human aggression or prove that cooperation always dominates. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
