# Limited Order Book

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 127 words

The scientific question is whether simple trading strategies and order matching generate market-level price behavior. In NetLogo's Limited Order Book model, traders with cash, token inventories and fixed value beliefs submit orders that are matched through a limit-order-book mechanism. The selected literature connects this mechanism to market microstructure and institutional effects: limit-order-book research connects matching rules to liquidity and prices [1]; zero-intelligence trading isolates the influence of market institutions [2]; experimental markets provide an empirical benchmark for competitive price formation [3]. Fixed beliefs, discrete scheduling and particular matching rules simplify real exchanges; comparison with empirical prices requires separately measured order-flow data. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
