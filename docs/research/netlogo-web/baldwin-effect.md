# Baldwin Effect

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can individual learning change the course of genetic evolution without acquired traits being inherited? NetLogo Baldwin Effect explores how learning helps organisms find successful behavior while reproduction changes a population's genetic composition. Baldwin's proposal [1], Hinton and Nowlan's computational study [2], and Maynard Smith's analysis [3] frame this question. The served Info identifies the Hinton–Nowlan approach as its starting point, with explicit extensions and parameter choices. Learning effort, genotype representation, and the chosen fitness environment are model assumptions rather than measured universal properties. Successful runs illustrate interaction between plasticity and selection. They do not imply Lamarckian inheritance, prove that learning always speeds evolution, or establish the effect's magnitude in a particular natural population.
