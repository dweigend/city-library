# Connected Chemistry Solid Combustion

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Connected Chemistry Solid Combustion investigates how energetic surface encounters control consumption of a solid fuel. Oxygen molecules collide with a block of carbon and react only when the model activation condition is met, releasing energy and producing carbon dioxide while nitrogen remains inert. Provides the activation-and-temperature perspective for reaction rates [1]. Reviews measured and modeled char-combustion rates, supplying a real heterogeneous-reaction comparison [2]. Provides a stochastic-reaction reference for interpreting discrete reaction events rather than deterministic bulk conversion [3]. The source models pure carbon and suppresses additional nitrogen chemistry; its collision rule, geometry and energy treatment are not a calibrated porous-char combustion model. The simulation illustrates the interaction between available surface, energetic encounters and changing reactant populations. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
