# Simple Birth Rates

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How does a small fertility difference change the composition of a bounded population? NetLogo Simple Birth Rates starts with red and blue agents, lets them reproduce at different rates, and randomly removes excess individuals above carrying capacity. The experiment illustrates selection through differential reproduction [1,3] while retaining chance extinction in a finite population [2]. Colors represent inherited types rather than explicit diploid genes, and the carrying-capacity correction gives every individual the same mortality risk. Consequently, dominance in one run does not establish a deterministic outcome or a universal selection coefficient. The simulation is an illustrative comparison of reproduction and sampling, not an empirically calibrated demographic forecast.
