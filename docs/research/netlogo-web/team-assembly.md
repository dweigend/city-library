# Team Assembly

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 118 words

The scientific question is whether newcomer recruitment and repeated collaboration generate different network structures. In NetLogo's Team Assembly model, new project teams combine newcomers and incumbents, with a tunable preference for past collaborators and removal of inactive participants. The selected literature connects this mechanism to collaboration-network assembly: the explicitly acknowledged team-assembly model supplies the core mechanism [1]; Broadway collaboration relates network structure to creative performance [2]; scientific coauthorship provides an empirical collaboration-network comparison [3]. Team-size and turnover rules simplify institutions; structural resemblance alone cannot establish that a particular team will be creative. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
