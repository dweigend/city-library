# Traffic 2 Lanes

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 129 words

The scientific question is whether lane switching changes the emergence and persistence of traffic jams. In NetLogo's Traffic 2 Lanes model, cars accelerate into free space, brake behind slower cars and may change lanes after repeated delays exhaust their patience. The selected literature connects this mechanism to microscopic traffic and lateral interaction: two-lane research isolates the importance of lane-changing logic [1]; a minimal freeway model connects local driving rules to congestion [2]; empirical traffic states provide a comparator for microscopic model behavior [3]. Patience, lane-change safety and vehicle motion are stylized rules; adding a lane-choice action does not by itself establish better throughput. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
