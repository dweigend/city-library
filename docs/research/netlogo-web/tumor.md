# Tumor

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can cell hierarchies affect tumor persistence after treatment? NetLogo Tumor distinguishes self-renewing stem cells from transitory cells with limited proliferation and eventual death. Its rules include asymmetric or symmetric division and a stylized secondary colony. Cancer hierarchy research [1,2] frames the contrast between persistent and short-lived populations, while spatial tumor modeling [3] highlights constraints on growth. The Info credits an original StarLogoT contribution by Gershom Zajicek. This is a qualitative demonstration with selected cell types and simplified movement, aging, and treatment rules. It illustrates how targeting one compartment can leave another intact, but does not validate clinical resistance mechanisms, estimate a patient's prognosis, or recommend cancer therapy.
