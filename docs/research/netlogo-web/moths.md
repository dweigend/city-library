# Moths

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Which local orientation rule could make insects circle a light? NetLogo Moths produces curved flight around a source by combining simple movement and orientation rules. The scientific question remains distinct from the visual resemblance: recent flight research [1], nocturnal-vision analysis [2], and light-pollution synthesis [3] constrain interpretation. The served Info explicitly discusses competing explanations and cites modern evidence. Its agents do not reconstruct full three-dimensional flight control, body orientation, or species-specific sensory processing. Circling therefore demonstrates what the implemented rule can produce. It does not establish that real insects navigate by that rule, resolve all attraction-to-light mechanisms, or quantify the ecological effects of a particular lighting installation.
