# Diffusion on a Directed Network

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 125 words

The scientific question is whether directed links concentrate an initially uniform conserved quantity into unequal node holdings. In NetLogo's Diffusion on a Directed Network model, each node sends a fixed fraction of its value equally through outgoing links, with topology constraining subsequent transfers. The selected literature connects this mechanism to directed transport and mixing: network random walks and diffusion contextualize directional transport [1]; agreement depends on interaction structure and communication assumptions [2]; directed normalized transfers connect to ranking methods with additional teleportation assumptions [3]. Outgoing-degree normalization, sinks and update timing must be specified; quantity transfer is different from independent disease transmission. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
