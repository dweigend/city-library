# Pursuit

Status: complete. Scientific research checked 2026-11-05.

How does always turning toward a moving target shape a follower's path? In NetLogo Pursuit, a leader advances along a selected mathematical function while followers repeatedly face the leader and move a fixed distance. Nahin's mathematical treatment of pursuit [1] gives the closest theoretical context. Differential geometry [2] clarifies the distinction between a target curve and the curves generated by followers, while Reynolds [3] supplies a related steering framework for autonomous motion. Step size and initial follower positions determine lag and path shape. The leader follows a prescribed, sometimes rescaled curve rather than responding strategically to pursuit. Updates are discrete and sensing is exact and instantaneous. The experiment illustrates feedback-driven trajectory construction; it does not establish interception guarantees, realistic animal pursuit, or optimal navigation under limited perception.
