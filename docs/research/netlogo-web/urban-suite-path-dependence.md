# Urban Suite - Path Dependence

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 127 words

The scientific question is whether early settlement events shape the eventual clustering of firms. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Path Dependence model, mobile firms probabilistically settle according to local quality and the number of firms already present, with alternative decision mechanisms. The selected literature connects this mechanism to historical contingency and spatial concentration: increasing returns explain persistent outcomes selected by historical events [1]; urban complexity models situate firm-location feedback in spatial systems [2]; reinforcement offers a comparator for uneven cumulative growth [3]. Location probabilities encode assumptions about agglomeration rather than estimating them; alternative random histories need ensembles before conclusions about lock-in. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
