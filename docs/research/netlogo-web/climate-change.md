# Climate Change

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 127 words

The scientific question is whether radiative absorption, reflection and escape change the Earths modeled energy balance. In NetLogo's Climate Change model, sunlight becomes stored heat or reflected energy, and emitted infrared packets interact with greenhouse-gas and cloud representations. The selected literature connects this mechanism to greenhouse forcing and energy balance: the original carbon-dioxide calculation connects atmospheric composition to temperature [1]; radiative-convective equilibrium provides a physically richer atmospheric comparison [2]; forcing efficacy qualifies simple equivalence between different climate perturbations [3]. Packet collisions and one surface temperature are teaching abstractions; the display does not resolve molecular spectroscopy, ocean circulation or regional climate change. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
