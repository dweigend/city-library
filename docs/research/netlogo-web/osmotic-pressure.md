# Osmotic Pressure

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Osmotic Pressure investigates how selective membrane permeability and particle populations produce osmotic effects. Solvent particles can cross a semipermeable divider while solute particles cannot; elastic collisions and the modeled membrane interactions alter the resulting balance. Provides the solution-gas analogy underlying dilute osmotic pressure [1]. Develops coupled solvent-solute membrane transport and its thermodynamic limitations [2]. Provides the kinetic collision framework reused from GasLab [3]. The source uses few particles and schematic solvent sticking at the membrane; chemical potentials, real solution interactions and membrane-specific coefficients are not independently calibrated. The simulation illustrates why selective accessibility and the number of solute particles matter more than a literal one-way flow rule. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
