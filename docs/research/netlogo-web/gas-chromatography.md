# Gas Chromatography

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Gas Chromatography investigates how unequal surface retention separates moving molecular populations. Two particle species wander downward through a porous medium; one sticks temporarily, delaying its arrival, while wall channels can bypass the packing. Introduces partition chromatography and its separation principle [1]. Analyzes diffusion and mass-transfer contributions to chromatographic band spreading [2]. Demonstrates gas-liquid partition separation in a concrete experimental application [3]. The baseline uses a fixed sticking delay and simplified downward motion instead of calibrated adsorption energetics, carrier-gas flow and column transport equations. The simulation illustrates retention-time separation and the way preferential channels undermine it. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
