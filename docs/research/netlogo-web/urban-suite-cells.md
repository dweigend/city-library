# Urban Suite - Cells

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 131 words

The scientific question is whether neighborhood and aging rules create different urban-like growth forms. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Cells model, cells activate probabilistically when neighboring active-cell counts satisfy selected rules and may deactivate after an age limit. The selected literature connects this mechanism to cellular growth and spatial rule sensitivity: the acknowledged source develops cellular approaches to urban form [1]; land-use cellular automata link local transition rules to spatial structure [2]; historical urbanization modeling illustrates the extra calibration required for applications [3]. The lattice has no households, transport costs or land market; visually urban patterns do not establish realistic land-use dynamics or planning effectiveness. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
