# Flocking Vee Formations

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can local visual and spacing rules produce V-like flight formations? NetLogo Flocking Vee Formations adapts flocking so birds approach visible neighbors, avoid obstructed views and excessive proximity, then match a nearby bird's speed and heading. The Info names Nathan and Barbosa's artificial-bird study as its loose source [1]. Empirical upwash research [2] and aerodynamic formation analysis [3] provide physical comparisons. The model uses a prescribed updraft distance but does not solve airflow or wingbeat energetics. V shapes and echelons illustrate coordination under limited vision and prioritized rules. Their appearance does not demonstrate measured energy savings, reproduce actual migratory behavior, or establish that a balanced formation will remain stable indefinitely.
