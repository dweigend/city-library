# Birthdays

Status: complete. Scientific research checked 2026-11-05.

Why do shared birthdays become common in surprisingly small groups? NetLogo Birthdays independently assigns each agent a day in a 365-day year and highlights agents sharing a birthday. Durrett's probability text [1] supplies the occupancy and collision framework, while Nunnikhoven [2] and Hurley [3] analyze the important extension to nonuniform birth frequencies. The model illustrates the difference between matching one specified person's birthday and finding any matching pair among many possible pairs. Repeated groups reveal sampling variation around theoretical probabilities. Uniform birthdays, independent assignments, and omission of leap day simplify the experiment. Real birth frequencies vary across dates and populations, so the classroom model is a controlled probability example rather than a demographic estimate. Agreement with its analytical collision probability tests the stated assumptions, not their empirical realism.
