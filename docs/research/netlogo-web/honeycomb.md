# Honeycomb

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can local movement feedback generate a honeycomb-like lattice? NetLogo Honeycomb has bees move in circles while leaving a diffusing, evaporating chemical; the chemical changes movement speed and turning radius. Its Info attributes these artificial rules to David Little and explicitly avoids claiming that real bees follow them. Scientific studies of comb organization [1] and physical cell transformation [2] provide biological comparisons, while the honeycomb theorem [3] addresses geometric efficiency. These are distinct explanations and should not be conflated. The simulation illustrates regular pattern formation from a chosen feedback loop. Hexagonal-looking output neither identifies the actual construction behavior of bees nor proves that this chemical mechanism optimizes real wax use.
