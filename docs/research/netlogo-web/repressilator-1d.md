# Repressilator 1D

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can a ring of inhibitory interactions generate repeated changes in gene expression? NetLogo Repressilator 1D represents DNA, mRNA, and regulatory proteins in a simplified three-component repression cycle. The original synthetic oscillator [1] supplies the direct scientific inspiration. Stochastic reaction methods [2] and later robust oscillator experiments [3] clarify the role of molecular noise and circuit design. The animation uses one-dimensional movement and selected production, binding, and degradation rules; it should not automatically be treated as an exact chemical master-equation implementation. Oscillations illustrate feedback with delay under these assumptions. They do not prove quantitative agreement with the original biological construct, establish parameter robustness, or reproduce every measured cellular time scale.
