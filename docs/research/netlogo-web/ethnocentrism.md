# Ethnocentrism

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 121 words

The scientific question is whether in-group favoritism can evolve through local competition and inheritance. In NetLogo's Ethnocentrism model, agents inherit group tags and separate cooperation decisions for same-tag and other-tag neighbors, with payoffs affecting reproduction. The selected literature connects this mechanism to conditional cooperation and evolutionary selection: the acknowledged ethnocentrism model directly specifies tag-conditioned cooperation [1]; reciprocity offers a contrasting route to cooperative behavior [2]; multiple evolutionary mechanisms delimit claims of one universal cooperation explanation [3]. Tags, mutation and local occupancy are stipulated; evolving favoritism in this environment does not explain every real ethnic conflict. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
