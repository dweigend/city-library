# Weak Acid

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Weak Acid investigates why weak-acid pH depends on dissociation rather than total acid alone. Acid molecules dissociate into hydronium and conjugate-base species and can recombine, letting the model track the balance between bound and free protons. Provides the proton-transfer framework for acids and conjugate bases [1]. Connects acid strength with maintenance of near-neutral conditions [2]. Quantifies acid-base response and buffering, clarifying limits of concentration-only intuition [3]. The baseline uses schematic molecular rules; activities, ionic-strength corrections and a measured solvent environment are not fully represented, so particle ratios do not automatically equal laboratory pH. The simulation illustrates how a small dissociated fraction can determine acidity despite a much larger reservoir of undissociated acid. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
