# Urban Suite - Positive Feedback

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 125 words

The scientific question is whether increasing returns amplify small differences in initial spatial activity. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Positive Feedback model, patch activity is raised to a tunable exponent, optionally after neighborhood averaging, and normalized relative to the current maximum. The selected literature connects this mechanism to reinforcement and unequal growth: the acknowledged source presents this particular nonlinear activity example [1]; reinforced growth supplies a classical skew-distribution mechanism [2]; cumulative advantage formalizes success-dependent expansion [3]. Normalization changes relative scale, and abstract activity has no fixed population unit; numerical dominance is not evidence for a specific urban economic process. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
