# Algae

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can an organism balance light near the surface against nutrients deeper in water? NetLogo Algae represents a vertical aquatic column where algae change density as light-driven storage and metabolism alter their buoyancy. Research on vertical migration [1], buoyancy structures [2], and competition under mixing [3] provides scientific context. The Info traces the model to an Allan Konopka workshop prototype. Its density bookkeeping, light distribution, and resource rules are simplified, and the model does not resolve lake hydrodynamics or full cellular physiology. Repeated movement illustrates one possible response to opposing resource gradients. It does not establish a calibrated migration cycle, explain all phytoplankton strategies, or predict a specific lake's bloom dynamics.
