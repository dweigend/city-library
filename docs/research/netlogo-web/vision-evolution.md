# Vision Evolution

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can changes in visual opportunity interact with a transition between water and land? NetLogo Vision Evolution represents vertebrate-like agents whose eye size affects perception across two environments, alongside changes relevant to terrestrial movement. The motivating hypothesis is explicitly attributed to MacIver and colleagues [1]. Eye-evolution modeling [2] and research on animal visual systems [3] provide comparisons for its assumptions. The simulation reduces optics, morphology, food detection, and inheritance to selected traits and rules. It illustrates how a hypothesized ecological opportunity could alter selection within that constructed setting. It does not reconstruct a fossil lineage, establish a unique causal sequence for terrestrial evolution, or show that larger eyes alone caused the origin of limbs.
