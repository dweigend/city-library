# Termites

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can random walkers organize scattered material into piles? NetLogo Termites uses agents that pick up encountered wood chips and deposit them beside other chips. Material already deposited changes what later walkers encounter, producing environmental feedback without a shared blueprint. Resnick documents the microworld lineage [1], while collective-sorting research [2] and stigmergy [3] explain its relevant mechanism. Chips remain removable, so piles can merge rather than becoming permanently protected clusters. The agents' rules are abstractions of transport and aggregation, not a detailed account of termite architecture or behavior. Pile formation illustrates decentralized coordination; its appearance alone does not validate a biological explanation or guarantee convergence within a specified time.
