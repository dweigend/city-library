# Membrane Formation

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can simple interactions between water and amphiphilic molecules create organized boundaries? NetLogo Membrane Formation represents lipids with connected hydrophilic and hydrophobic parts, plus water particles. Attraction and repulsion can produce collective structures without an imposed membrane template. The Info connects this approach to artificial-chemistry research [1,2]; amphiphile self-assembly theory [3] supplies a physical comparison. The interaction selection and movement rules are computational approximations rather than a molecular force field with calibrated units. Resulting aggregates illustrate how local preferences can organize space. Their appearance alone does not establish thermodynamic equilibrium, realistic bilayer elasticity, or a complete account of biological membrane formation, which also depends on composition and environmental conditions.
