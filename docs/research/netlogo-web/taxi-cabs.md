# Taxi Cabs

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 127 words

The scientific question is whether fleet size, spatial demand and fares change taxi utilization and profitability. In NetLogo's Taxi Cabs model, taxis cruise a zoned road grid, pick up waiting passengers and travel to requested destinations using Manhattan-distance routing. The selected literature connects this mechanism to spatial taxi-market supply and demand: network taxi modeling connects cruising and passenger service [1]; fare and fleet regulation interact with spatial demand equilibrium [2]; agent-based taxi simulation provides a directly comparable modeling approach [3]. Stylized districts, pickup encounters and routing omit dispatch platforms and many driver decisions; simulated earnings require local cost and demand calibration. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
