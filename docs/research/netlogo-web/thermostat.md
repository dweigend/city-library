# Thermostat

Research status: complete. Literature research only; implementation acceptance is separate.

NetLogo Thermostat investigates how local measurement and on/off control regulate a leaky heated room. Mobile heat-representing agents enter from a central heater, escape through a partially permeable boundary, and are sampled by a thermometer that controls heating. Establishes dynamical feedback-control questions beyond simple target matching [1]. Provides the modern feedback and stability framework for interpreting sensor-driven switching [2]. Connects measurement and corrective action within a general control framework [3]. Heat agents are explicitly a pedagogical representation rather than literal heat particles; sensor sampling, leakage and switching rules determine the apparent response. The simulation illustrates feedback, fluctuations, heat loss and the consequences of measuring only locally. Reproducing these patterns establishes rule fidelity, not empirical validation.
