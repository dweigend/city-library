# Urban Suite - Recycling

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 124 words

The scientific question is whether resource-restoring behavior can persist against faster but destructive exploitation. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Recycling model, recyclers take partial resources or restore wasted cells, while wasteful agents exhaust cells and all agents maintain energy budgets. The selected literature connects this mechanism to resource feedback and collective sustainability: shared-resource dilemmas contextualize individually profitable depletion [1]; institutional evidence challenges assumptions that depletion is inevitable [2]; resource-use research connects management choices with longer-term sustainability [3]. Arbitrary gains, restoration costs and regeneration multipliers stand in for complex material cycles; neither label measures a real households environmental impact. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
