# Daisyworld

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can organisms regulate an environment without intending to do so? NetLogo Daisyworld couples black and white daisies to temperature: their different albedos alter local heating, while temperature changes their ability to reproduce. Watson and Lovelock provide the original feedback argument [1]; subsequent analyses place its assumptions and spatial extensions in context [2,3]. The served NetLogo version makes local growth, heat diffusion, and changing luminosity observable. Its two daisy types, stylized growth response, and simplified energy balance are deliberate reductions. A temperature plateau demonstrates a possible feedback mechanism under these rules, not evidence that Earth's climate necessarily self-regulates or that evolutionary processes always stabilize environmental conditions.
