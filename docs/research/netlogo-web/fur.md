# Fur

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can short-range activation and longer-range inhibition generate different skin-like patterns? NetLogo Fur uses a lattice of differentiated and undifferentiated pigment cells. Weighted contributions from inner and outer neighborhoods determine whether cells change state. Turing supplies the general pattern-forming idea [1], and Young supplies the local-rule variant identified by the Info [2]. Experimental mammalian patterning research [3] illustrates how related mechanisms can be tested biologically. The simulation assumes fixed neighborhood geometry and reduced cell states, without explicit molecular concentrations, tissue growth, or species-specific genetics. Spots and stripes demonstrate the repertoire of this rule system. Their resemblance to real fur is not empirical validation of the implementation or proof of a universal pigmentation mechanism.
