# Urban Suite - Structure from Randomness 2

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 138 words

The scientific question is whether persistent threshold activation turns fluctuating local potentials into lasting spatial patterns. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Structure from Randomness 2 model, each patch averages its previous potential with four neighbors, adds random signed noise and remains activated after crossing a threshold. The selected literature connects this mechanism to noise, local coupling and threshold memory: the acknowledged source gives this specific potential-threshold example [1]; urban cellular models provide a comparison for local spatial interaction [2]; historically grounded urban simulation highlights what empirical application additionally requires [3]. An irreversible display state differs from the underlying reversible potential; the model omits resource budgets, actual buildings and observed urban development data. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
