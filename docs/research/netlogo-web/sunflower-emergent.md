# Sunflower Emergent

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can a spiral arrangement emerge without prescribing the golden angle? NetLogo Sunflower Emergent adds particles on successive rings and selects positions by minimizing an approximation of repulsive energy. The Info describes a Stenger-inspired construction and relates it to physical phyllotaxis [1]. A geometric sunflower model [2] and a developmental model [3] offer useful comparisons. The simulation treats growth as sequential point placement with chosen energy rules; it does not represent a complete plant meristem or auxin transport. Double spirals illustrate the consequences of spatial exclusion and growth under these assumptions. They do not establish that repulsion alone explains every plant pattern or that a matching image confirms a biological mechanism.
