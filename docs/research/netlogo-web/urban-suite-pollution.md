# Urban Suite - Pollution

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 132 words

The scientific question is whether pollution diffusion and local mitigation alter population persistence. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Pollution model, power plants maintain pollutant sources, trees reduce nearby pollution and moving people lose health or reproduce depending on their condition. The selected literature connects this mechanism to coupled environmental stress and demographic feedback: toxicant-population models formalize exposure-dependent persistence and extinction [1]; coupled population dynamics supply a classical ecological comparison [2]; embodied ecological models clarify links between individual rules and population outcomes [3]. Pollution is an abstract field, and health decline is a stipulated exposure rule; tree effects are not calibrated removal rates or epidemiological risk estimates. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
