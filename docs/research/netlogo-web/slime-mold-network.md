# Slime Mold Network

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can distributed responses to nutrient signals produce a transport-like network? NetLogo Slime Mold Network approximates Physarum exploration through local movement and signal-dependent growth or withdrawal. Its Info describes biological tube flow as motivation, while the implemented approximation belongs to a reduced computational model. Particle-network research [1], signal-propagation studies [2], and adaptive-network experiments [3] frame what is illustrated. The simulation compresses cytoplasmic hydraulics, contractions, and material transport rather than resolving them directly. Network-looking patterns show how local rules can redistribute exploration toward resources. They do not establish that every visible strand is a physical tube, prove network optimality, or validate quantitative flow and growth predictions for an actual organism.
