# Fireflies

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

When can locally interacting flashers synchronize their rhythms? NetLogo Fireflies represents individuals with internal flash timing and rules that advance or delay their cycle after observing neighbors. Biological accounts of synchronized flashing [1] and communication [2] motivate the example, while pulse-coupled oscillator theory [3] supplies a mathematical comparison. The simulation makes the effect of population density, timing rules, and interaction range visible. Its discrete counters and simplified perception omit species-specific signal recognition, sex differences, and measured response curves. Synchrony in a run illustrates coordination without a central clock. It does not imply that every firefly species synchronizes or that the assumptions of a continuous oscillator theorem hold exactly in this NetLogo implementation.
