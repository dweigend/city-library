# CRISPR Ecosystem

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How does adaptive bacterial immunity reshape competition between bacteria and viruses? NetLogo CRISPR Ecosystem models interacting populations with innate and CRISPR-mediated protection, allowing comparison of ecological and evolutionary consequences. Ecological CRISPR theory [1] and empirical studies of phage evolution [2,3] provide mechanism-specific context. The served model describes a tiny seawater-like setting, but uses simplified encounters, growth, resistance, and viral change rather than a calibrated microbial community. The simulation illustrates feedback between host defense and viral pressure, and how population outcomes differ when adaptive memory is available. It does not demonstrate a universal coexistence rule, quantify real marine abundances, or validate all of the molecular and ecological assumptions through visual agreement alone.
