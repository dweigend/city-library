# Fairy Circles

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can interacting processes create regular vegetation patterns at several scales? NetLogo Fairy Circles is inspired by a theory combining competition among termite colonies with vegetation feedback. The Info explicitly cites Tarnita and colleagues [1]; termite evidence [2] and spatial vegetation research [3] contextualize the mechanism and competing explanations. The simulation represents simplified colonies, resource effects, and patterned grass rather than a site-calibrated desert ecosystem. It illustrates how distinct local processes can interact to produce organized spatial structure. Rings and regular spacing are not sufficient to identify the true cause of every observed fairy-circle landscape. The model therefore supports comparison of assumptions and patterns, while field measurements and independent tests remain necessary for causal attribution.
