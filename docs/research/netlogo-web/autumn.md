# Autumn

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do changing environmental conditions produce visible autumn leaf colors? NetLogo Autumn represents the production and loss of pigments, then connects their relative presence to leaf appearance and falling. Research on senescent-leaf anthocyanins [1], seasonal pigment biochemistry [2], and vegetative anthocyanins [3] supplies the relevant biological context. The served model combines effects of sunlight, temperature, and water through simplified rules. Its colors summarize underlying chemistry rather than resolve optical spectra, species-specific pigment pathways, or measured weather responses. Varying the conditions illustrates how several processes can contribute to a seasonal pattern. A plausible display is not validation of pigment concentrations, an explanation of every species' autumn colors, or a forecast of the timing of leaf fall.
