# epiDEM Travel and Control

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do mobility and control measures change the spread of infection? NetLogo epiDEM Travel and Control extends the basic epidemic with travel, isolation, quarantine, inoculation, and individual links. Classical epidemic theory [1], transport-linked epidemic modeling [2], and intervention analysis [3] frame these mechanisms. The served Info assumes no viral mutation and perfect immunity after recovery, which limits the diseases and time scales the example can represent. Its contact and control rules are illustrative abstractions, not fitted estimates of compliance, vaccine protection, or real travel flows. Comparing scenarios exposes consequences of these assumptions. It does not establish policy effectiveness for an actual outbreak or replace empirical evaluation of interventions and uncertainty.
