# L-System Fractals

Status: complete. Scientific research checked 2026-11-05.

How does symbolic rewriting become geometric form? NetLogo L-System Fractals expands a string using production rules and interprets the symbols as turtle movement and turning. Repeated rewriting generates increasingly elaborate figures from short descriptions. Lindenmayer's original paper [1] establishes the developmental grammar approach, its companion paper [2] extends the treatment to branching filaments, and the Algorithmic Beauty of Plants [3] connects these formalisms to geometric turtle interpretation. The model illustrates recursion, rule composition, and the relationship between a symbolic description and its spatial realization. Chosen angles, step scales, production rules, and iteration depth determine the result. A finite rendering cannot realize an infinite fractal, and a plant-like drawing does not model physiology or establish a biological developmental explanation without additional measured assumptions.
