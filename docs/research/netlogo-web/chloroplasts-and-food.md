# Chloroplasts and Food

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do light-dependent energy transfer and carbon fixation connect in photosynthesis? NetLogo Chloroplasts and Food depicts a single plant cell, with simplified chloroplast processes that connect water, carbon dioxide, energy carriers, and sugar formation. Carbon-fixation research [1], quantitative assimilation modeling [2], and chloroplast oxygen experiments [3] place these representations in scientific context. The served model is a curricular sequence of interacting components, not a full biochemical reconstruction of the Calvin cycle or electron transport. Molecule counts and encounter rules are teaching abstractions rather than measured concentrations or rate constants. It illustrates dependencies among inputs and outputs; visual agreement with a pathway diagram does not establish physiological accuracy or predict plant productivity.
