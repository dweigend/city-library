# Bacterial Infection

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How does antibiotic exposure change the composition of a bacterial population? NetLogo Bacterial Infection represents bacteria with different membrane porosities, reproduction when space is available, and antibiotics entering through membrane openings. Permeability research [1], resistance population genetics [2], and evidence on resistance costs [3] provide the scientific context. The served curricular model compares stylized treatment environments and makes differential survival visible. Its pores are an explicit teaching abstraction, not a complete representation of resistance mechanisms, pharmacokinetics, or mutation pathways. A population becoming harder to eliminate illustrates selection under those rules. It does not validate a clinical treatment schedule, establish that all resistance works through porosity, or quantify outcomes for an individual patient.
