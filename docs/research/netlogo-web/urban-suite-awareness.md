# Urban Suite - Awareness

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 130 words

The scientific question is whether information encounters and interpersonal influence can sustain or extinguish awareness. In NetLogo's Urban Suite - Awareness model, moving people gain points from information centers or informed neighbors, lose points otherwise and create new centers after reaching an activist threshold. The selected literature connects this mechanism to reinforcement, decay and social influence: experiments distinguish behavioral diffusion from simple information transmission [1]; threshold participation contextualizes the transition to modeled activism [2]; social-influence mechanisms delimit interpretation of interpersonal persuasion [3]. Awareness points, fixed gains and threshold activism are invented operational measures; the simulation does not estimate actual recycling attitudes or advertising effects. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
