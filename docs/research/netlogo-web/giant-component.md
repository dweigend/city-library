# Giant Component

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 113 words

The scientific question is whether random edge addition creates a sharp change in large-scale reachability. In NetLogo's Giant Component model, initially isolated nodes receive uniformly selected new edges, and connected components merge as the graph becomes denser. The selected literature connects this mechanism to random-network connectivity: finite random graphs exhibit the birth of a giant component [1]; network structure determines component and reachability properties [2]; rewired local networks offer a contrasting connectivity mechanism [3]. Finite-size fluctuations, undirected edges and uniform mixing restrict extrapolation to structured human contacts. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
