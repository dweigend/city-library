# Mammoths

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can hunting pressure and environmental deterioration contribute to extinction? NetLogo Mammoths contrasts simplified hunting and climate-change explanations for megafaunal loss. An overkill simulation [1], evidence relating warming to population turnover [2], and research combining climate and human influences [3] provide complementary scientific contexts. The model uses stylized populations and environmental rules to make possible causal pathways visible. Its historical dates and narrative should be checked against the publications rather than treated as current chronological consensus. Extinction in a run demonstrates a consequence of chosen assumptions, not a settled attribution of real mammoth disappearance. Parameter sensitivity and the interaction of pressures matter, and the simulation alone cannot distinguish among competing historical explanations.
