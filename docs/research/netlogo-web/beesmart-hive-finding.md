# BeeSmart Hive Finding

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How can scouts collectively choose a nest site without a leader comparing every option? NetLogo BeeSmart Hive Finding represents the actively searching minority of a honeybee swarm. Scouts discover sites, communicate support, and contribute to a collective decision through simplified behavioral rules. Seeley's synthesis [1], quorum-sensing experiments [2], and stop-signal research [3] supply the relevant scientific context. The Info explicitly notes modifications and the omission of most non-scout bees. Its spatial layout, quality assessments, and decision rules are abstractions rather than a complete reconstruction of a field swarm. A chosen site illustrates distributed evidence accumulation under those assumptions, not proof that the colony always finds the globally best option or that every signaling mechanism is represented.
