# Disease Solo

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How does movement behavior alter opportunities for infectious contact? NetLogo Disease Solo combines one user-controlled person with computer-controlled agents, and infection spreads through encounters. Options for avoidance and pursuit let users explore how behavior changes exposure. Classical epidemic dynamics [1], awareness–disease feedback [2], and intervention modeling [3] provide scientific context. This is a one-player derivative of the HubNet Disease activity, not a fitted surveillance model. Its android strategies and simplified infection rule deliberately isolate contact effects without reconstructing an actual population's social network or pathogen biology. Different outbreak patterns illustrate consequences of movement choices within the simulation. They do not establish real intervention effectiveness or predict infection risk for an individual.
