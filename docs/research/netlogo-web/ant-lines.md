# Ant Lines

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Why can a chain of followers trace a smoother route than its leader? NetLogo Ant Lines sends a leader toward food along a wandering path; each later ant heads toward the position of the ant ahead. Bruckstein's mathematical study [1] is the explicit model inspiration. Pursuit dynamics [2] and experimental tandem running [3] provide complementary comparisons. The served Info itself says biological accuracy is uncertain. Delays, turning, and repeated local following generate a geometric filtering effect without a chemical trail or global route plan. The simulation illustrates a mathematical mechanism for path smoothing, not a validated account of ant navigation, evidence of teaching by its agents, or a proof of optimal foraging routes.
