# Ants

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

Can a colony exploit food sources using only local chemical information? NetLogo Ants combines wandering search with pheromone deposition by ants carrying food home. Other ants follow the chemical field, while diffusion and evaporation continually modify it. Research on ant shortcuts [1], exploratory patterns [2], and trail-dependent path choice [3] provides mechanism-specific context. The served model emphasizes reinforcement and depletion rather than individual route maps or centralized allocation. Its chemical grid, movement rules, and simplified food piles omit species-specific sensing and recruitment details. Efficient-looking paths illustrate a possible collective effect of local feedback. They do not prove global optimality or establish that the model quantitatively reproduces a real colony.
