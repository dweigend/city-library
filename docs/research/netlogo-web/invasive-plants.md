# Invasive Plants

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How do local spread and treatment interact across neighboring farms? NetLogo Invasive Plants assigns each patch an infestation level, permits spread, and applies weed-killer treatment with effects extending to nearby patches. The Info identifies Auld and Coote as a loose source [1]; dispersal theory [2] and invasion research [3] provide broader scientific context. The model makes spatial externalities and repeated intervention visible without representing individual seeds, crop economics, or chemical transport in detail. Its bounded infestation scale is an abstraction rather than a measured density. Comparing interventions illustrates consequences of the chosen spread and treatment rules, not demonstrated field effectiveness, pesticide safety, or a forecast for a named invasive species.
