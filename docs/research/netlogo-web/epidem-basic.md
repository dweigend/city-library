# epiDEM Basic

Status: complete. Scientific research only.

How does an initially rare infection spread through a susceptible population? NetLogo epiDEM Basic is explicitly based on the Kermack–McKendrick epidemic framework [1], but makes individual movement and infection events visible. Susceptible, infectious, and recovered states evolve in a closed population with no births, deaths, or travel. Community-size research [2] and stochastic reaction methods [3] help distinguish finite random realizations from deterministic aggregate equations. These references contextualize the model; they do not imply that its stepping algorithm is Gillespie's method. The simulation illustrates depletion of susceptible individuals and outbreak variability. Its stylized contacts, fixed biological assumptions, and uncalibrated parameters do not establish a forecast for any named disease or community.
