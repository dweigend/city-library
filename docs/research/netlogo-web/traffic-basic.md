# Traffic Basic

Status: **complete** · Checked: 2026-11-05 · Mini-text: 125 words

The scientific question is whether stop-and-go waves can emerge without an external road obstruction. In NetLogo's Traffic Basic model, cars circulate on a single road loop, accelerating into free space and braking when a car is close ahead. The selected literature connects this mechanism to endogenous congestion waves: minimal local vehicle rules generate congestion in freeway models [1]; a circular-road experiment observes jam formation without bottlenecks [2]; empirical traffic-state analysis gives a richer microscopic comparison [3]. A closed homogeneous road and simplified following rule omit merging and driver diversity; the simulation is distinct from a fitted empirical traffic model. The original NetLogo behavior is the baseline for reimplementation. This simulation offers a controlled thought experiment, and agreement with its output does not establish empirical validation.
