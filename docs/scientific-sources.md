Synthetic teaching rules remain defined by the model contracts. Bibliographic records below retain their recorded access limits; metadata verification does not imply full-text access. DOI metadata were rechecked on 11 November 2026. Publisher abstracts remain the access level for Rosenthal (2014) and Batty and Xie (1994); author-hosted original PDFs were retrieved for Leslie (1945), Braess et al. (2005), Luo and Wang (2003), and Draper and Lund (2004). Retrieval and text extraction do not imply a complete new reading or scientific validation.

The catalog research JSON, original sources, notices, hash manifests and independent reports are preserved evidence. They are not a second maintained reader bibliography.
