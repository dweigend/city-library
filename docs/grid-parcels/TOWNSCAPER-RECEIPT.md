# Parcel and finite-topology source provenance

The original design package came from `dweigend/city_game` commit
`5583c03039b1937cfe6d388b54692e583c2cdc26`. It transferred documentation,
not runtime implementation or acceptance. The complete original package and its
28-file checksums remain available at the immutable City Library revision below.

[Original proposal](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/cc6d5c3136c07cda94d5a4449b6e384c435f6ad4/docs/library-handover/parcel-proposal/README.md),
[catalogue and proposed signatures](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/cc6d5c3136c07cda94d5a4449b6e384c435f6ad4/docs/library-handover/parcel-proposal/api-reference.md),
[original transfer and rights record](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/cc6d5c3136c07cda94d5a4449b6e384c435f6ad4/docs/grid-parcels/TOWNSCAPER-RECEIPT.md),
[original package checksums](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/cc6d5c3136c07cda94d5a4449b6e384c435f6ad4/docs/grid-parcels/TOWNSCAPER-PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS).

Use the [current API](API.md), [finite-topology contract](QUAD-LAYOUTS-CONTRACT.md)
and [status](STATUS.md) for delivered behavior and remaining work. The historical
49-row catalogue, 58 signature sections and 27 type/result rows remain design
requirements, not delivered exports. Their numerical and dependency decisions
require separately frozen examples before implementation.

The source prototype has no identified permissive license; no source implementation
was copied. Historical publication figures are attributed to Vanegas et al. (2012),
Aichholzer et al. (1995), Parish and Müller (2001), and Chen, Song and Ortner (2024).
The copied raster images have been removed; the original publication links and
attribution remain in the fixed-revision package. Rasterization was not license
clearance. No redistribution rights, runtime fidelity or fresh review approval
are implied.
