# Flat DES scenes and elevator historical acceptance

This record summarizes the earlier flat-scene/elevator work before the repository's `models/` consolidation. The [original acceptance record](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/754e4cc0bb1afb5b417aeda1d918d3b654c8c85f/docs/development/des-2d-evidence.md) is pinned to feature revision `754e4cc`; its paths, commands and two-host descriptions are historical. The [current main integration record](pr-main-integration.md) owns new merged-source checks.

The ten DES mechanisms retained their original model behavior while their scenes adopted flat state-driven primitives. The eleventh model adds a synthetic elevator with model-owned directional queues, Resource capacity and Agenda events. Its maintained [contract](des-elevator-contract.md) and [pinned primary-source research](../research/des-elevator-sources.md) distinguish mechanism adaptation from physical elevator certification.

Recorded checks include a full pre-late-fix chain of 737 tests and 699,395 assertions, all registered examples, build and packed consumers; focused later geometry/label checks and a clean typecheck/UI build; eleven real browser flows; and independent inspection of 44 final screenshots. An independent refactor preserved 55 complete observation/scene pairs byte-for-byte. Native and Gemini source reviews approved the bounded source scope separately from browser/image acceptance.

The source review identified DOM canvas dimension wrapping and the image review found a narrow depot-label overlap. Both were corrected and checked in their affected scope. The [original source review](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/754e4cc0bb1afb5b417aeda1d918d3b654c8c85f/docs/development/des-2d-gemini-review.md), [browser record](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/754e4cc0bb1afb5b417aeda1d918d3b654c8c85f/docs/development/des-2d-browser-review.md) and [image approval](https://github.com/dweigend/city-library/blob/754e4cc0bb1afb5b417aeda1d918d3b654c8c85f/docs/development/des-2d-image-review.md) preserve the exact commands, source heads and method limitations. They do not approve later model-path or host integration automatically.
