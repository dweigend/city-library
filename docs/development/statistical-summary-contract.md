# Statistical summaries

Models should share basic measurements rather than carry private statistics helpers. `stats.describe(values,options?)` summarizes an iterable with count, sum, range, mean, median and spread. Economic Disparity and the central-limit experiment supply the first use cases. The exact target signature is in [the statistics reference](../agent-loom/api-reference.md#complete-statistics).

Public `stats.describe(values:Iterable<number>,options?:{sample?:boolean}):Summary`, samplefalse default. Export readonly Summary through src/stats/index.ts and the root index. Return frozen count,sum,min,max,mean,median,variance,standardDeviation. Empty count/sum0,otherfieldsundefined; singleton population spread0; sample spread undefined at n<2. Consume a finite iterable once,copy before sorting for median,preserve caller order. Even median is mean of middle pair. Population variance divides centered squared deviations by n,sample by n−1. Reject nonnumeric/nonfinite inputs,malformed/unknown/accessor options and nonfinite arithmetic,including sum or spread overflow; no mutation/RNG/World/renderer effect. Follow existing histogram/gini input-validation/error conventions. Validate options before iterating values.

Public examples in `tests/public-contracts/summary/` cover: unsorted population/even median;sample and degenerate values;one-shot iterable and detached output;invalid/options/overflow rejection;model-facing median and CLT sample spread. Preserve accepted syntax/expectations and existing stats API. Numerical formulas remain one owned pure kernel; consuming models select domain quantities and call it rather than maintaining private summary libraries. No stubs or speculative APIs.
